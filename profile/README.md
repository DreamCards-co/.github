**DreamCards** is an AI assistant for Amazon and Shopify sellers. It audits product listings and rewrites their text and images.

- **Audit:** a score for each listing, with the reasons and what to fix first.
- **Rewrite:** titles, descriptions, and images, in English and other European languages.
- **You decide:** every action shows its cost up front, and nothing is published without your approval.

We're building it now. Follow along at [dreamcards.co](https://dreamcards.co).
